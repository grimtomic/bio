AI is an intresting topic, while it can be an amazing assistive tool it isn't without its flaws.
insert dead internet theory
Alot of the content you will see online such as blogs posts, news articles, and social media posts are generated by AI.
Hell even this content your reading right now has an ai agent assisting me with my code editor helping with massively repetitive tasks, for as long as thats going to be a free feature...
In a vacuum this may not be an issue, but when a study starts using a souce that was hallucinated by an ai, and future models are trained on the same 'fake' data.
You might think its not that big of a problem, but lets look at whats happening in the medical field where ai is being used to diagnose conditions, where "no accute findings" where found at 4x the rate compared to older generations of the same model.

Now while that may not be relevant to you there are other issues. To which a paper from MIT is important.
In essence, it comes down to to maybe no shock to some, that using ai personally to complete your work doesnt actually teach you anything.
The study asked 3 groups to write an essay on the same topic. One group having access to an ai agent, second access to a search engine, and the last group only has access to thier brain. During the study, the brain activities of the participants were recorded.
I recommend reading the paper and drawing your own conclusions, but in my crude summary it showed most brain activity in the memory retention part of the brain, in the no help group, less in the browser group, and least in the ai group.
In my eyes this would speak towards the groups with the higher brain activies forming more neural connections and retaining more memory, and such a capactity for reasoning on the topic.
This is in the same way a coder with just a text editor will think and reason about code, knowing how to do things,
another one who would look at places such as stack overflow for help, would be able to adapt others code but would have a harder time reasoning and solving issues, but still learning along the way
and finally a vibe coder who would be hopelessly reliant on the ai not learning, and unable to solve issues on their own.
